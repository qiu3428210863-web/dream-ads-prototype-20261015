04 A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you

Do I need to be a professional creator?+

No. Designers, AI artists, content creators, and anyone with a big advertising idea can join. Bring your curiosity and a product you want to reimagine.

Can I use an imaginary product?+

Yes. Choose a real or imaginary product and build an original campaign around it.

What should my submission include?+

One original video ad of around 15 seconds made with OpenArt, plus an English creative concept of up to 100 words.

When does the challenge start?+

Exact dates will be announced. The challenge lasts 14 days from the opening of submissions. This is a fictional design exercise; there is no live competition or prize claim.
